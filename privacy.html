Pocket Wisdom

Privacy Policy

Effective 30 September 2026

Pocket Wisdom is a short-video page that publishes one reel a day on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook. The Pocket Wisdom publishing app ("the app") is an internal tool used only by the Pocket Wisdom owner to upload our own videos to our own accounts and to read the performance of those videos. It is not offered to the public and no one else can sign in to it.

What the app accesses

  • Our own account details from each platform: account or channel ID, username and display name.
  • Access tokens that each platform issues when we sign in, so the app can post on our behalf.
  • Performance data for our own videos: views, watch time, likes, comments count, shares, saves and follower counts.

The app does not collect data about viewers of our videos beyond the aggregated statistics each platform provides. It does not read private messages, contacts or anyone else's content.

How it is used

Only to upload and schedule Pocket Wisdom videos, set their captions, and measure which videos perform best so we can make better ones. We do not sell, rent or share this data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Tokens and settings are stored on the owner's own computer. Video statistics are stored in a private database (Supabase) that only the owner can access. Video files are hosted temporarily on Cloudflare R2 so platforms can fetch them.

Third-party services

The app uses the YouTube API Services, the TikTok Content Posting API, and the Meta Graph API for Instagram and Facebook. By using YouTube features you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke the app's access to a Google account at any time at Google security settings. TikTok and Meta data is handled under the TikTok Privacy Policy and the Meta Privacy Policy.

Data deletion

To have any data the app holds about an account deleted, email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} with the account name. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email. Removing the app from your account settings on Google, TikTok, Instagram or Facebook also stops all access immediately.

Retention

Video statistics are kept for as long as the Pocket Wisdom page is active. Tokens are deleted when access is revoked.
